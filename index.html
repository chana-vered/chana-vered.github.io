About

A teacher who begins with the human being in front of her.

Chana Vered is a passionate Torah educator, spiritual mentor and inspirational speaker. Her teaching starts where life actually happens — in anxiety, in transition, in the ordinary weeks that ask more of us than we thought we had.

Drawing on decades of Jewish thought alongside meditation, mental health awareness and energy healing, she translates ideas that can feel abstract into something you can use before the day is over. Her classes are warm, unhurried and honest, and they leave people with tools rather than inspiration alone.

She teaches internationally, mentors privately, and speaks to communities, schools and organizations looking for depth without heaviness.

“The wisdom was never meant to stay on the page. It was meant to change the way you walk through your week.”
